Delete account – Hively

As of: 8 August 2026

If you want to delete your Hively account and the associated cloud data, you can request this at any time by email.

How to request deletion

  1. 1 Send an email to hively.support@proton.me with the subject Delete account – Hively.
  2. 2 Provide the email address you used to sign in to the app.
  3. 3 You will receive a confirmation once the account and cloud data have been deleted — within 30 days at the latest.
Send account deletion email

What is deleted

DataDeletion
Account (email, password hash) Fully deleted
Cloud-synced operation data Fully deleted
Active Hively Pro subscription Cancelled; Stripe invoices may be retained as required by law
Important: Purely local data on the device is not affected by account deletion. Use “Delete local data” in the app or uninstall the app.
