Privacy policy – Hively

As of: 8 August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Hively app (Web/PWA as well as the native iOS and Android apps, package name ch.hively.app) and explains which personal data we process, for what purposes, and what rights you have.

2. Legal basis

The processing of your personal data is primarily governed by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Where you use the app from the EU, we additionally follow the principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. Core principle: local-first

Hively is designed as a “local-first” app: your hive, inspection, harvest, and finance data are stored primarily locally on your device and work fully offline. An account is optional and only required if you want to sync data across devices or use Hively Pro.

Anonymised usage data Product improvement PostHog (EU servers, eu.i.posthog.com). No autocapture; Session Replay only when explicitly enabled (inputs masked)

5. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the processors named above, to the extent required for the respective purpose.

6. Retention period

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is stored for as long as your account exists and is removed upon account deletion. Stripe retains invoice data in accordance with statutory retention obligations.

7. Your rights

  • Access to the personal data processed about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your account and associated cloud data (instructions)
  • Provision of your cloud data in a common format (data portability) – on request by email to the contact address; you can delete local data in the app settings on the device
  • Withdrawal of a given consent (e.g. location access) at any time with effect for the future

8. No advertising

Hively shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDKs.

9. Children

Hively is not directed at children under 13 years of age and does not knowingly collect personal data from children in that age group.

10. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (including encrypted transmission and access restrictions at processors).

11. Changes

We update this policy as needed. The current version is always available on this page.
