Hively is the app for beekeepers who want to manage their apiaries right at the hive stand – quickly, clearly, and even without an internet connection.

APIARIES & COLONIES
Organize multiple apiaries and record colonies with queen, breed, year, and health status. The dashboard shows colony count, honey harvest, balance, and weather and pollen data (bee radar).

LOG INSPECTIONS
Capture observations for one or more colonies, including weather at inspection time. Full history per colony is always available.

TREATMENTS
Document varroa treatments with product, period, and harvest waiting time.

SEASON CALENDAR
A monthly plan with text checklists helps you stay on top of the beekeeping year.

FINANCES AND YIELDS
Keep expenses, honey harvests, and colony sponsorships under control.

TEAM & SPONSORSHIP
Optional sign-in: invite collaborators or give read-only access to sponsors. Joining by invite code is free.

WORKS OFFLINE
All core data is stored locally – the app works without network at the apiary. With sign-in: optional cloud backup across devices.

HIVELY PRO (OPTIONAL)
With Hively Pro (14-day trial, CHF 1.99/month or CHF 10/year) and sign-in:
• AI voice assistant – understands Swiss German, writes notes in standard German
• Receipts read automatically from photos
• AI weather insights and per-colony recommendations
• Cloud sync and team invite creation

AI features require internet and a Hively Pro subscription.

Hively is for hobby and part-time beekeepers who want paperless, digital beekeeping management.
