Hively is the app for beekeepers who want to manage their apiaries right at the hive stand – quickly, clearly, and even without an internet connection.

APIARIES AT A GLANCE
Record all your colonies with queen, breed, year, and health status. The dashboard shows colony count, honey harvest, finances, and current weather and pollen data (bee radar).

LOG INSPECTIONS
Capture observations for one or more colonies, including weather at inspection time. Full history per colony is always available.

HANDS-FREE DICTATION
The AI voice assistant captures your notes in Swiss German – ideal when your hands are busy at the apiary.

RECEIPTS BY PHOTO
Snap receipts and let AI extract expenses automatically – no more manual typing.

FINANCES AND YIELDS
Keep expenses, honey harvests, and colony sponsorships under control.

SEASON CALENDAR
A monthly task plan helps you stay on top of the beekeeping year.

WORKS OFFLINE
All data is stored locally – the app works without network at the apiary. Optionally sign in and sync across devices via the cloud.

Hively is for hobby and part-time beekeepers who want paperless, digital beekeeping management.
